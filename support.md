# Support And Refunds

Policy date: 2026-10-10. This notice applies to IncomeMate 1.0.0, the free native iOS app.

Publisher: Abdullah Rahimy, an individual in Louisiana, USA.

Contact: [Email IncomeMate support](mailto:incomemate26@gmail.com) at incomemate26@gmail.com.

## Contact Support

Use email for app problems, privacy questions or help understanding backup and deletion controls. There is no in-app support form or IncomeMate account. Email goes through your chosen mail service to the publisher; it is not an automatic ledger upload. Support does not remotely disable your app or offline data access.

## Send A Safe Report

- Describe what happened and what you expected, using fictional transactions, categories and names.
- Include the app version, device model and iOS version only if useful and voluntarily supplied. Serial numbers, device identifiers and account identifiers are not needed.
- Give reproduction steps using made-up values. Remove real financial details and identifying information from any attachment, including visible text and image metadata.
- Never send backup passphrases, bank credentials, database files, unredacted receipts or financial screenshots. Support will never request them.

Minimize any sensitive information you voluntarily include. If you have already emailed it, contact support to request removal. Removal is subject to applicable obligations and the limits of copies held by mail providers or recipients; it is not a guaranteed erasure of every copy.

## Statement Import Problems

Check the selected ledger and currency. Choose a source from Files or Photos, select Read Statement, then review each row's date, description, amount, category, method and source. Inspect and edit uncertain fields before checking valid rows and selecting the Import button showing the checked-row count to save them. For unknown layouts or partial or unsupported pages, try a clearer source or use manual entry. Source processing is local, but extraction is not guaranteed to be complete or accurate. Describe the issue using fictional labels and a small synthetic text fixture with made-up values; never email a real statement, full source PDF or image, bank credentials or account/card details. Support will not request them.

## Support Privacy And Retention

The publisher receives your sender email address, name if supplied, message, attachments and limited technical information you deliberately provide. These are used to respond, troubleshoot and handle justified legal or security obligations. Support email is not used for marketing, and no consent checkbox is required to email support.

Gmail/Google processes support mail and may store or process it outside your country under its service arrangements. No specific EU/UK processor basis, international-transfer safeguard or compliance certification is claimed by this notice.

Support information is retained only as long as needed to handle the request and justified legal/security obligations. This is a retention criterion, not a fixed period or automatic-deletion guarantee. Contact the support email about access, correction, deletion or other rights concerning publisher-held information, subject to applicable law and its response requirements.

## Free Release And Refunds

IncomeMate 1.0.0 is free, with no paid download, in-app purchases or IncomeMate subscriptions. There is no IncomeMate app payment to refund in this version. Insights "Subscriptions" is recurring-pattern detection, not billing.

If a paid download or in-app purchase billed by Apple is separately introduced later, request a refund through [Apple's refund request service](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) or consult [Apple's refund guidance](https://support.apple.com/118223). Apple handles and decides requests under the relevant law, storefront and purchase rules. The publisher cannot promise approval or categorically refuse every refund. No fixed refund window is stated here, and mandatory consumer rights remain available. This notice does not approve a future paid offering.

## Data Recovery And Notice Availability

The publisher cannot recover a lost .imbk passphrase or retrieve local records it has never received. The current .imbk archive does not include receipt image bytes or an attachment index. Preserve independent image copies; do not rely on the archive to restore receipts. Photos, shared files and device or cloud backups have separate retention and deletion controls.

The importer cannot recover transient source content after finish, cancellation, backgrounding or app lock. Schema-9 encrypted .imbk backups preserve the transactions you selected and saved and their statementImportID values, not statement PDFs or source images; the receipt-image limitation above remains.
