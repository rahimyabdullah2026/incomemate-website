How to use IncomeMate

Everything runs locally on your iPhone in six straightforward steps.

Step 1

Track your ledger at a glance

Open the Dashboard to see monthly or yearly Income, Expense, and Balance totals alongside a spending-by-category chart.

  • Switch between Monthly and Yearly totals in one tap.
  • Filter recent transactions by All, Income, or Expense.
Step 2

Add manually or attach a receipt

Tap + to enter an amount, choose Income or Expense, pick a category, and optionally attach a receipt from Camera, Photo Library, or Files.

  • Apple Vision reads receipt fields locally for your review before saving.
  • Includes built-in categories like Salary and Refund / Side Income, plus custom ones you create.
Step 3

Speak multiple entries & edit inline

Tap the microphone to speak several entries in one go. Everything queues up cleanly so you can review before saving.

  • Tap the mic again anytime to append more entries to the same list.
  • Tap any queued entry’s category to change it or flip Income/Expense before tapping Save All.
Step 4

Import bank & card statements locally

Choose Import Statement from the + menu and pick a PDF or statement photos. IncomeMate reads them on your device and sorts both Income and Expenses by month.

  • Filter by All, Income, or Expense, and batch-assign categories or payment methods with Apply to all.
  • Card payments and internal transfers stay excluded by default so you never double-count.
Step 5

Set monthly targets & review drafts

Use Insights to set category spending targets, spot repeating patterns, and review Siri drafts before they post.

  • See actual spending vs. target progress for every category each month.
  • Manage active and stopped recurring bills without altering past history.
Step 6

Organize, lock & back up your data

Open Settings to switch ledgers, customize categories with visual icons, set Light/Dark mode, and turn on reminders or Siri drafts.

  • Protect your data with optional Face ID / passcode App Lock.
  • Create password-encrypted .imbk backups or export any date range to CSV.
