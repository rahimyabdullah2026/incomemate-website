# IncomeMate

A private, offline personal finance tracker for iPhone. Your financial records stay on your device: no account, no cloud sync, no ads, and no analytics.

## Features

- Track income and expenses with exact amounts, categories and payment methods.
- Import transactions from a bank statement (PDF or images), read on your device.
- Multiple ledgers, each in its own currency.
- Recurring transactions, spending targets and Reports.
- Encrypted backups and optional App Lock with Face ID.
- Voice entry, receipt capture and Siri draft entry.
